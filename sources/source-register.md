## Analytical cautions

1. Sector investment figures are commitments reported through GOGLA's methodology and may not equal cash disbursed in the same period.
2. Company announcements are issuer-reported and are not substitutes for transaction documents or audited portfolio data.
3. Securitisations, receivables facilities, corporate debt and combined debt/equity packages are structurally different and should not be compared as equivalent instruments.
4. Company examples illustrate categories; inclusion is not an investment recommendation.
5. Figure 7 (operating rail) and Figure 8 (vertical/instrument map) are analytical frameworks rather than measured datasets.
