# The Trojan Rail: Africa's Asset-Finance Infrastructure

Public research repository accompanying Kenneth S. Legesi's article on how PayGo solar built a distributed operating rail for asset finance across Africa's informal economy.

## Read the publication

- **Published article:** https://legesi.xyz/research/paygo-trojan-rail-africa/full-report/
- **Project overview:** https://legesi.xyz/research/paygo-trojan-rail-africa/
- **Original discussion:** Walter Tukahirwa's LinkedIn post, https://lnkd.in/p/eSjSrEku

## Research question

Did PayGo solar companies merely become lenders, or did they build an origination, servicing, collections and device-control infrastructure capable of financing assets beyond solar?

## Principal finding

The durable asset is the closed-loop operating rail:

**Distribution + alternative underwriting + asset control + digital collections + servicing + loan-tape reporting.**

Receivables vehicles and local-currency funding can finance the assets produced by that rail, but they do not eliminate origination or servicing risk.

## Data and interpretation

- Sector totals are primarily from GOGLA's annual investment reporting.
- Market access and capital-requirement estimates are from World Bank / ESMAP / GOGLA.
- Company transaction sizes are drawn from public company or financing-partner announcements.
- Conceptual diagrams and opportunity classifications are Oakstone Capital analytical synthesis.
- Disclosed transaction sizes are not necessarily comparable across securitisations, receivables facilities and combined debt/equity packages.
